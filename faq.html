REAL CUSTOMER QUESTIONS

Clear answers before you enquire.

These answers reflect AAAM's current profile and strategic positioning without claiming unsupported achievements.

What does AAAM EVENTS do?

AAAM EVENTS is a Madurai-based full-service event organizing company established in 2015. Its profile covers planning and coordination, decoration, entertainment, stalls, food counters and event execution for family, wedding and professional programmes.

Does AAAM only do birthday decoration?

No. The profile includes wedding and traditional ceremonies, family celebrations, corporate and professional events, reception and wedding stalls, food court stalls, entertainment, decoration and theme setup.

What wedding services are available?

Engagement, reception, Muhurtham assistance, Haldi, Seer/Aarthi plates, Nadaswaram/Chendamelam, pooja arrangements, customized entry concepts, stalls, decoration and entertainment can be discussed based on the event requirement.

What corporate services are current?

Product launch, corporate mela/customer meet, annual day, school/college events, store opening and commercial decoration, sales-day counters and exhibition stall setup are shown in the current AAAM profile.

Does AAAM provide MICE services?

MICE is a strategic growth area. AAAM is building toward broader conference and MICE capability, but this website does not claim large established MICE experience without real portfolio evidence.

Can I customize my event?

Yes. The service model is designed around customized proposals. You can select the function and experience layers you need, then AAAM can prepare a proposal based on venue, guest count, theme and execution requirements.

Does AAAM offer fixed packages?

Package tiers can be used as a planning framework, but the final proposal is customized. The strategy specifically treats pricing and profitability calculations as a private business layer rather than public fixed claims.
